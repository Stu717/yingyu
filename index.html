邀请信写作复习

① 介绍自己

I am Li Ming, a student from ____ and one of the organizers of this activity.

我是李明，来自____的学生，也是本次活动的组织者之一。

I am Li Ming, a student representative from ____.

我是李明，来自____的学生代表。

I am Li Ming, ____ of ____.

我是李明，____的____（如：主席/负责人）。

② 写信目的 + 活动信息

写信目的：

It is my great pleasure to invite you to our annual forum.

On behalf of the Student Union, I would like to invite you to deliver a lecture.

活动信息（时间/地点）：

..., which will be held in the school auditorium at 2 p.m. on June 15th.

in Meeting Room 201 at 3 p.m. next Friday

③ 活动价值（句式框架）

The activity will provide you with a good opportunity to ______.

The activity will enable you to ______.

The activity will be a good chance for you to ______.

④ 适度恭维收信人

As an expert in this field, you are widely respected by our students.

With your rich experience in this area, you would be a most welcome guest.

Your presence would be greatly appreciated.

⑤ 明确请求 + 截止时间

We would be grateful if you could confirm your attendance before June 10th.

Please let us know whether you can come by June 10th.

⑥ 客套话

Should you have any questions, please do not hesitate to contact me.

We look forward to welcoming you to our university.

Thank you for your time and consideration.

Thank you in advance for your support.

⑦ 期待回复

Looking forward to your reply.

We look forward to hearing from you soon.

⑧ 活动类型短语库（配合第③部分使用）
▶ 学术交流类（会议/讲座/论坛）

交流观点exchange ideas with other scholars

分享研究share your research findings

了解前沿learn about the latest developments in the field

▶ 指导点评类（比赛/答辩/评审）

指导年轻学生offer guidance to young students

点评作品comment on their performances

发现人才discover talented students

▶ 社交拓展类（晚会/聚会/社团）

结识新朋友make new friends

扩大社交圈meet people with similar interests

与师生交流communicate with our students and teachers

▶ 放松娱乐类（晚会/典礼/庆祝）

放松身心relax and enjoy yourself

欣赏节目enjoy wonderful performances

与学生同乐spend a pleasant evening with us

▶ 展示分享类（讲座/经验分享/演讲）

分享经验share your valuable experience

发表见解present your ideas

展示才华show your talent

▶ 了解合作类（开放日/校企/学术会议）

了解学生learn more about our students

促进合作promote cooperation with our university

建立联系build closer ties with us
